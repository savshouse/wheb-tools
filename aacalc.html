Money Purchase Annual Allowance (MPAA)

The tax year will be split at this date. MPAA applies to DC contributions after this point.
⚠️
MPAA applies from the trigger date. DC/money purchase contributions after this date are limited to the MPAA (currently £10,000). Carry forward cannot be used to increase DC contributions above the MPAA.

DB accrual is unaffected — an Alternative Annual Allowance (standard AA minus MPAA = £50,000 currently) applies to DB savings, and carry forward can be used against this.

In the trigger year, enter your DC and DB inputs separately using the split fields below — pre-trigger uses normal rules, post-trigger applies the MPAA.

Step 2 — Tapered Annual Allowance

ℹ️
Enter the tapered Annual Allowance already calculated for each relevant year. Leave blank to use the standard allowance for that year.
ℹ️
How this works: Work through each tax year below. Enter total income and other figures — the tool calculates threshold income first. If that exceeds the limit, it asks for the remaining figures to compute adjusted income and your client's actual Annual Allowance for that year. Hover the ? icons for guidance on each field.

Key rule: Both threshold income and adjusted income must be exceeded for the taper to apply. If threshold income is within the limit, you can stop there for that year.
⚠️
Important: If further employer contributions are planned, recalculate — additional contributions increase adjusted income and may further reduce the AA. The maximum employer contribution in a tapered situation is approximately two-thirds of the remaining AA shown.

Step 3 — Historical pension inputs

ℹ️
Enter pension inputs for each year shown. When the MPAA applies, the table splits into DC (money purchase) and DB (defined benefit) columns — DC is tested against the MPAA, DB against the alternative Annual Allowance. Only carry forward from the DB side is available once the MPAA is triggered. Note: 2008/09–2010/11 inputs are capped at £50,000 for carry forward purposes.
Tax year Annual Allowance Total pension inputs (£) Unused allowance AA excess
Cumulative AA available = this year's Annual Allowance + unused allowance from the 3 preceding tax years (FIFO — oldest consumed first). A year contributes zero unused if contributions equalled or exceeded that year's AA, but this does not affect unused amounts from other years. A year where inputs exceeded the AA but the excess was fully absorbed by carry forward from earlier years is not a taxable excess year. Taxable excess ⚠️ = inputs that exceed the AA plus all available carry forward for that year — this is what would give rise to an Annual Allowance charge. When MPAA applies: DC contributions are tested against the MPAA only — carry forward cannot increase the DC cap. Carry forward can only be used against the alternative Annual Allowance (DB/pre-trigger side). Carry forward is only available from years where the client was an active member of a registered pension scheme.
