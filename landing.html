How it works

Three simple steps to compete against fellow ONs' members all season long.

1

Sign up & pick your squad

Create your manager account, then choose your team of 10 from the full ONs' player pool within the budget cap.

2

Score points every week

Your players earn points from their real match performances — tries, penalties, Man of the Match and more.

3

Compete all season

Make transfers, set your captain, and chase the top of the leaderboard. Points accumulate across the whole season.

The rules

Built around the club's own fixtures — forwards, backs, and every XV counts.

Your squad

  • 10 players total — 7 starters + 3 bench
  • Captain scores double points
  • Vice-captain covers if captain didn't play
  • Bench auto-subs for no-shows

Transfers

  • 2 free transfers per week
  • Deadline: Friday at 22:00
  • Players from all ONs' XVs eligible
  • Price balances performance differences

Points

  • Based on real match statistics
  • Season points accumulate each week
  • Weeks lock once officially scored
  • Prize fund split across top finishers

Terms & conditions

▼

ONs' Fantasy Rugby Manager is run by Old Northamptonians RFC for the enjoyment of club members. Participation is open to all paid-up members of the club at a cost of £10 per season.

By signing up you agree to your name and profile picture being shared with other managers and associated club social media channels. You confirm you are a genuine paid-up member of Old Northamptonians RFC.

The competition organisers reserve the right to remove any participant who is not a current paid-up member, or who uses offensive language or imagery in connection with their account. Entry fees are non-refundable in such cases.

Points are calculated from official match statistics entered by club administrators. Scores are final once a gameweek is locked. The organisers' decision on any dispute is final.

The prize fund is formed from all entry fees collected. Distribution across top-placed managers and players will be confirmed before the season begins.
