❔ How the game works — tap for a quick guide

1. Pick your squad. Choose 7 starters (4 forwards + 3 backs) and 3 bench players, all within your budget. No position rule on the bench — pick anyone.

2. Pick a captain and vice-captain. Your captain's points are doubled that week. If your captain doesn't play in any of the three matches, your vice gets the double instead.

3. Your team carries over each week. You don't start from scratch — your squad rolls forward automatically. You just make changes if you want to.

4. Transfers are limited. You get 2 free transfers a week. Swap more than that in one go and the save will be blocked — so plan your changes rather than rebuilding the whole team.

5. Deadlines lock your team. Picks close Friday at 22:00, once team sheets are out. After that your squad is locked until the next gameweek opens.

6. No-shows are covered. If one of your starters doesn't play in any of the three matches that week, your first bench player who did play automatically steps in and scores for you instead. No action needed from you.
