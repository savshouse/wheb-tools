How these figures are calculated — assumptions & methodology

Pot accumulation: Your current pension pot grows annually using the growth rate you set, compounding after your and your employer's contributions are added each year. Your salary increases each year by the salary growth rate, and any contribution tiers you've added will change the contribution percentages from the age specified.

Other savings: ISA or other savings pots grow at the same growth rate as your pension and increase each year by the annual contribution you specify.

At retirement: Your pot is split into tax-free cash (up to 25%), an optional annuity purchase (using the annuity rate you set), and a remaining drawdown pot. The annuity income is treated as fixed (or inflation-linked if selected) for life.

Drawdown & exhaustion: Your drawdown pot pays out your chosen fixed monthly amount (or staged amounts if set), continuing to grow at your specified drawdown growth rate. If withdrawals exceed the remaining pot, the pot is exhausted and the “drawdown exhausted” age is shown. Guaranteed income sources (state pension and other income) continue regardless of drawdown pot exhaustion.

State pension & guaranteed income: Applied from the start age you set, optionally increasing with your inflation assumption each year. Other guaranteed income sources (e.g. DB pensions) are added from their own start ages.

Income tax: Estimated using 2024/25 UK tax bands and the standard personal allowance (£12,570), basic rate (20%), higher rate (40%) and additional rate (45%). This is a simplified estimate and does not account for personal allowance tapering, Scottish tax rates, or other individual circumstances.

Monte Carlo simulation: Runs 500 randomised scenarios, varying the annual growth rate by ±4% and the drawdown pot's growth by ±3% each year to reflect real-world investment volatility. The shaded bands show the range of likely outcomes; the percentage of scenarios in which the pot is exhausted is shown beneath the chart.

Inflation & real values: Figures labelled “real” or “today's money” are adjusted for the inflation rate you set, to show purchasing power rather than the nominal future amount.

This planner is a guide to support discussion and does not constitute financial advice. All projections are estimates based on the assumptions above, which may not reflect actual future performance, tax rules, or personal circumstances.
